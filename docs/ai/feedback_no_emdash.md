# No Em Dashes

> No em dashes or long-dash connectors in messages and reports for the team

Do not use em dashes (—) or similar long-dash connector punctuation in reports, status updates, Slack/email-style messages or any text written for the team. Use periods, commas, colons, or words like "with", "since", "so".

**Why:** A standing preference from the team, repeated after it recurred, so it needs active checking on every draft.

**How to apply:** Proofread the full text for the — character before sending, including quick follow-ups. This applies to messages, not to the working paper itself, whose wording belongs to the author.
