# Plain Language

> Keep replies plain and short; the people reading them are often not technical

Conscious Compute rule: replies are plain and simple. Short, everyday English, no jargon, no long explanations.

**Why:** Words like branch, PR, commit, deploy, metadata, schema, component, cache or API mean nothing to a non-technical reader and make the tool feel unusable.

**How to apply:** Lead with the answer in 1 to 3 sentences. Skip the mechanism unless asked. Offer choices as a short list, not a wall of trade-offs.
